<h2>准备</h2>
<h3>前提</h3>
<p>一般移动适配都需要设置视口</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #0000ff;">&lt;</span><span style="color: #800000;">meta </span><span style="color: #ff0000;">name</span><span style="color: #0000ff;">="viewport"</span><span style="color: #ff0000;"> content</span><span style="color: #0000ff;">="width=device-width,initial-scale=1,user-scalable=no,maximum-scale=1,minimum-scale=1"</span><span style="color: #0000ff;">&gt;</span></pre>
</div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h2>简单适配</h2>
<h3>原理</h3>
<p>页面用rem作为单位，html定义font-size，随着窗口的变化，动态设置font-size。</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h3>技巧</h3>
<p>1、<strong>设置font-size：</strong>要求容易换算</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #000000;">html{
    font-size: 15px;
}</span></pre>
</div>
<p>2、<strong>获取固定系数：</strong>设计稿宽度 / font-size = 固定系数</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">若设计稿1200px宽，font-size为15px</span><span style="color: #008000;">
//</span><span style="color: #008000;">所以 1200 / 15 = 80；</span>
<span style="color: #0000ff;">var</span> ratio = 80; </pre>
</div>
<p>3、<strong>获取不同屏幕宽度：</strong>用实际的屏幕宽度 代替 设计稿宽度</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;"> 获取屏幕宽度</span>
<span style="color: #0000ff;">var</span> viewWidth = document.documentElement.clientWidth || window.innerWidth || document.documentElement.getBoundingClientRec().width;</pre>
</div>
<p>4、<strong>动态设置font-size：</strong></p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">设置html的font-size</span>
document.documentElement.style.fontSize = viewWidth / ratio + 'px';</pre>
</div>
<p>5、<strong>窗口发生变化就改变font-size：</strong>将上面代码封装到函数里</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">页面加载时，触发一次</span>
<span style="color: #000000;">setFont();
</span><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">当宽口发生变化时，再触发一次</span>
window.addEventListener('resize',setFont);    </pre>
</div>
<h3>&nbsp;</h3>
<h3>实际操作</h3>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #0000ff;">function</span><span style="color: #000000;"> setFont(){
    </span><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;"> 获取屏幕宽度</span>
    <span style="color: #0000ff;">var</span> viewWidth = document.documentElement.clientWidth || window.innerWidth ||<span style="color: #000000;"> document.documentElement.getBoundingClientRec().width;
    </span><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">定义一个固定的系数</span>
    <span style="color: #0000ff;">var</span> ratio = 27.24<span style="color: #000000;">;
    </span><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">设置html的font-size</span>
    document.documentElement.style.fontSize = viewWidth / ratio + 'px'<span style="color: #000000;">;
}
</span><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">页面加载时，触发一次</span>
<span style="color: #000000;">setFont();
</span><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">当宽口发生变化时，再触发一次</span>

</div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h3>缺点</h3>
<p>不能解决1px边框问题。就是在dpr为1的时候边框是1px，dpr为2时边框为2px，dpr为3时边框为3px。</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h2>通用适配</h2>
<p>//在简单适配的基础上修改</p>
<h3>原理</h3>
<p>设置了边框为1px，但是在dpr为3的时候就变成了3px。我们可以在meta的视口标签上更改页面的缩放比就可以解决。如dpr为1时缩放比为1；dpr为2时缩放比为1/2；dpr为3时缩放比为1/3。</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h3>技巧</h3>
<p>1、获取viewport的meta标签：</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #0000ff;">var</span> viewport = document.querySelector('meta[name=viewport]');</pre>
</div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>2、获取当前的dpr值：</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #0000ff;">var</span> dpr = window.devicePixelRatio;</pre>
</div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>3、定义scale值：</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #0000ff;">var</span> scale = 1 / dpr;</pre>
</div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>4、更改viewport里面的scale值：</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">自定义一个contennt，里面用上面计算出的scale</span>
<span style="color: #0000ff;">var</span> cont = 'width=device-width,initial-scale='+ scale +',user-scalable=no,maximum-scale='+ scale +',minimum-scale='+<span style="color: #000000;"> scale;
</span><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">更改meta标签里面的content</span>
viewport.setAttribute('content',cont);</pre>
</div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>5、禁止font-size无限制被放大 / 缩小：（可选）</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">定义最大宽度和最小宽度（这里定义的是不带dpr的宽度）</span>
<span style="color: #0000ff;">var</span> maxWidth = 540 , minWidth = 320<span style="color: #000000;">;
</span><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">若屏幕宽度比max-Width大，则viewWidth等于max-Width</span>
<span style="color: #0000ff;">if</span>(viewWidth &gt; (maxWidth*<span style="color: #000000;">dpr)){
    viewWidth </span>= maxWidth*<span style="color: #000000;">dpr;
}
</span><span style="color: #008000;">//</span><span style="color: #008000;">若屏幕宽度比min-Width小，则viewWidth等于min-Width</span>
<span style="color: #0000ff;">else</span> <span style="color: #0000ff;">if</span>(viewWidth &lt; (minWidth*<span style="color: #000000;">dpr)){
    viewWidth </span>= minWidth*<span style="color: #000000;">dpr;
}</span></pre>
</div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>

<h3>实际操作</h3>
