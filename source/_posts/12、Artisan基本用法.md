<p>一、创建控制器（最后一个参数为控制器名）</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre>php artisan make:controller StudentController</pre>
</div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>二、创建模型（最后一个参数为模型名）</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre>php artisan make:model Student</pre>
</div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>三、创建中间件（最后一个参数为中间件名）</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre>php artisan make:middleware Active</pre>
</div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>四、查看更多命令</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre>php artisan list</pre>
</div>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>五、运行当前 laravel 项目</p>
<div class="cnblogs_code">
<pre>php artisan serve</pre>
</div>
